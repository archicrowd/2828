The common legacy fund

Public debt and pension promises from the old system remain each state's responsibility. The blueprint offers a common fund with a common interest rate. It spreads the burden until repayment in 2098 and is financed by a surcharge on all taxes.

Pension rule BS35: promises free up to an allowance max(1 G; min(insurance years, 35)/35 × 2 G), 27.5 % up to the allowance + 1 G and 55.1 % above; own savings not reduced; pensioners' health and care contributions disappear.
